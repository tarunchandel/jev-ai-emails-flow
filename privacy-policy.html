Privacy Policy

Application: Jev AI Corporate Actions Flow (com.genaiapps.jevaiflow)
Effective Date: October 3, 2026 • Version: 1.0.1
Key Privacy Guarantee: Jev AI Corporate Actions Flow is designed with privacy and operational confidentiality at its core. The mobile application operates with local execution and demo sandbox datasets, and does not collect, track, or sell your personal information or contact details.

01. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information through the Jev AI Corporate Actions Flow mobile application (the "App") developed by GenAi Apps ("we", "us", or "our").

The App serves as an intelligent corporate actions email parsing, document OCR, and decision workflow platform powered by TypeSafe AI's Jev System One decision engine and Google Gemini.

02. Information Collection and Processing

We adhere to strict data minimization principles:

🚫 No Personal Identification

We do not collect names, phone numbers, email contact lists, or account credentials from your device.

🔒 Local Demo Environment

Benchmark financial corporate actions data is processed in a standalone local sandbox on the mobile device.

📍 No Geolocation Tracking

The App does not access, query, or log your precise or coarse geographic location.

🚫 No Advertising Identifiers

The App contains zero advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or third-party behavioral analytics.

03. Device Permissions & Technical Data

The App requests only the technical permissions necessary for its intended functionality:

  • INTERNET (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to communicate with optional backend endpoints, retrieve software updates, and execute remote AI decision requests when Live API mode is explicitly engaged.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely to detect whether the device is currently online or offline, dynamically activating the Standalone Offline Demo Mode.

04. Artificial Intelligence and Data Transmission

When utilizing live decision engine features:

  • Corporate action notifications are processed using enterprise-grade API connections protected by Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 encryption).
  • Data processed via our integration with TypeSafe AI (Jev System One) and Google Gemini is utilized exclusively for real-time document OCR and structured decision generation, and is not utilized to train general public models.

05. Google Play Services & Diagnostics

As distributed through the Google Play Store, anonymous technical crash reports and installation telemetry may be aggregated by Google Play Console services to help us ensure stability, backwards compatibility across Android versions, and bug resolution.

06. Children's Privacy

The App is designed as a financial operations and enterprise productivity tool targeted at adult business professionals (ages 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

07. Security Measures

We implement administrative and technical security measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. All cryptographic application bundles are signed using industry-standard RSA certificate signing.

08. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
